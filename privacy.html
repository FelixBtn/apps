Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data our Atlassian Marketplace apps, including Mermaid Diagrams with AI, process, why, and what rights you have. It also covers our help center and this website.

Who we are

The publisher and data controller is Félix Boittin, entrepreneur individuel (micro-entrepreneur), France. Full details are in the legal notice. Contact us through our help center or at contact@clivio.app.

The short version

  • The app runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure (Atlassian Forge) and sends no data outside Atlassian.
  • Your diagrams stay in your Confluence site. We, the publisher, cannot see them.
  • The app uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Data processed by the app

DataWhere it is keptPurpose
Diagram code and settingsIn your Confluence pages, as part of the macroDisplay and edit your diagrams
Export images of diagrams (PNG)As attachments of the page in your Confluence siteShow diagrams in PDF and Word exports
Text sent to the AI features (your description, the diagram code, a syntax error)Processed by Atlassian-hosted models (Forge LLMs) to answer the request; not stored by the appGenerate, fix or explain a diagram when a user asks
AI usage counters: number of AI requests and of model tokens per site and monthApp storage on Atlassian's infrastructure; contains no personal data and no contentApply monthly AI limits and monitor costs
Technical logs: event types, token counts, error codes, timestampsAtlassian's Forge logging; no diagram contentDetect and fix errors

The app reads and changes Confluence content only as the signed-in user and only when that user acts (for example, converting code blocks or saving a diagram). It does not read user profiles, email addresses or other content.

AI requests are processed by Atlassian under its own terms and privacy commitments, with the same content moderation as Atlassian's own AI features. See Atlassian's privacy policy.

Data we receive as the publisher

  • Licensing information from Atlassian: the licensed site, licence type and dates, and the technical and billing contacts the customer gave Atlassian. Purpose: licensing and support. Legal basis: performance of the contract and our legitimate interest in running our business.
  • Support requests: your name, email address and the content of your requests, stored in our help center (Jira Service Management, hosted by Atlassian). Purpose: answering you. Legal basis: performance of the contract or our legitimate interest in providing support. Kept for up to 3 years after your last request.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on this site.

Recipients and transfers

We do not sell or share data. Our service providers are Atlassian (app hosting, AI models, Marketplace, help center) and GitHub (this website). They may process data outside the European Union under their own safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention

Diagrams and export images stay in your Confluence site until you delete them. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's storage (AI usage counters) after 28 days. Support requests are kept as described above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Contact us through the help center or at contact@clivio.app. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to your local authority.

Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.
