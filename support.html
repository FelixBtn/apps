Support

We help with installation, usage, bugs and licensing questions for our apps.

How to reach us

Open a request on our help center: https://felixb-support.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/1. Sign in or sign up with your email address, then choose Question, Problem or Suggestion. You can follow your requests and reply to us from the same place.

To help us answer quickly, include your Confluence site address (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net), what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot. If a diagram does not render, paste its Mermaid code.

Response times

PriorityExamplesFirst response
CriticalThe app stops working for everyone on your site, or a security issueWithin 24 hours
NormalA bug with a workaround, a usage question, a licensing questionWithin 5 business days, usually much sooner

Business days follow the French calendar (Europe/Paris time zone). Support is provided in English and French.

Billing and licences

Purchases, trials, invoices, renewals and refunds are handled by Atlassian through the Atlassian Marketplace. For billing questions you can also contact Atlassian.

Security issues

Please report vulnerabilities as described on our security page.
